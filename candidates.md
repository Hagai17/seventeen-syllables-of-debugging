# Candidates — Seventeen Syllables of Debugging

Thirteen candidate haiku were drafted, every syllable counted word by word and verified by script, and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria in order; the round-1 winner then defended its place against three fresh challengers in a revision round.

**Final winner (5-7-5, 74 chars):**
Ship day: I strip out
each print line I used to hunt
one rides along, live

**Runner-up (5-7-5, 74 chars):**
Who wrote this garbage
I ask the screen at midnight
git blame says my name
